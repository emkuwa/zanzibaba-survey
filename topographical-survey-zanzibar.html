When do you need a topographical survey?

It is commonly requested before architectural design, drainage planning, road or access design, site grading, landscaping and other development work where existing ground levels and features matter.

It can also help a project team understand slopes, access conditions and the relationship between existing structures and the proposed development. The survey scope should match what the architect, engineer or consultant actually needs.

Typical survey information

Depending on the agreed scope, the survey may capture ground levels and contours, buildings and structures, roads and access, walls and visible site features, drainage features and other relevant physical details.

Deliverables are confirmed per project according to the intended architectural, engineering or planning use.

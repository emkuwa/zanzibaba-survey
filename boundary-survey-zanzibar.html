When boundary verification is useful

Consider a boundary survey before purchasing land, installing a perimeter wall or fence, beginning architectural design, setting out a new building or where the physical occupation of a plot needs to be checked against available survey information.

Send us the location and copies or photos of the land documents you have. We can then determine what information is available and the appropriate survey scope.

What to send for a quotation

1. Google Maps pin or plot location
2. Approximate plot size
3. Available survey plan/title/land documents
4. Why you need the boundary checked

Final requirements and deliverables depend on the assignment and applicable procedures.

Accuracy and equipment depend on the specification

We do not publish one universal marine-survey accuracy or claim one instrument for every assignment. Suitable equipment, survey lines, positioning, water-level/tide treatment, checks and processing depend on the intended use and technical requirements.

Marine work may also depend on access, vessel arrangements, weather and sea conditions, site hazards, permissions and operational requirements. Navigation-critical or specialist assignments can require specific standards and should be identified at enquiry stage.

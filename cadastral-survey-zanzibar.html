Start with the purpose of the survey

A request involving a land parcel may concern boundary information, subdivision, existing survey records, a land transaction or another administrative or development need. These situations should not all be treated as the same survey assignment.

We first review what you need to achieve and the information available for the plot. The appropriate survey scope can then be identified.

Cadastral survey, boundary verification or subdivision?

If your main concern is checking the relevant plot positions on the ground, start with our boundary verification service. If you intend to divide land into smaller plots, see plot subdivision survey support.

If you intend to design or develop the site, a topographical survey may also be needed to provide site levels and existing physical features to your design team.

Official and legal requirements

Surveying can provide measured land information, but legal ownership, registration, title processing, approvals and statutory acceptance depend on the applicable procedures and responsible authorities. We do not promise government approval or legal outcomes through this webpage.

Survey services are carried out with qualified surveying professionals according to the requirements of each assignment. Where a specific certification, submission or statutory process is required, that requirement should be confirmed for the individual case before the scope is agreed.

Survey Requirements and Compliance in Zanzibar

Survey requirements vary according to the type of land, project, intended use of the survey and any applicable administrative or statutory process.

Important: This page is general information and is not an official statement of Zanzibar law or a substitute for instructions from the responsible authority. Current legal, registration, licensing, coordinate-system, submission and approval requirements should be confirmed for the specific assignment.

Why the purpose of the survey matters

A topographical survey for architectural design is not necessarily subject to the same deliverables or process as a land parcel survey, subdivision, boundary matter or engineering construction survey. The survey brief should therefore identify what the information will be used for.

Documents and professional requirements

Where an assignment depends on land records, project drawings, official submissions or certification, the relevant documents and professional requirements should be identified before work starts. We do not promise statutory approval through this webpage.

Technical requirements

Coordinate reference, accuracy, control, deliverable format and verification requirements should be selected according to the project brief and any applicable authority or consultant specification rather than treated as one universal standard for every survey.

Our approach

Survey services are carried out with qualified surveying professionals according to the requirements of each assignment. If a particular registration, certification or authority submission is required, tell us the purpose of the survey and provide the relevant brief or checklist so it can be reviewed before quotation.

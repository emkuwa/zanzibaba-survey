Construction Setting Out Explained

Setting out transfers the required positions, axes, reference points and levels from project drawings onto the construction site.

What is needed before setting out?

The surveyor needs the latest approved-for-use project information relevant to the work. Depending on the assignment this can include site plans, architectural or engineering drawings, coordinates, grid information, levels and existing survey control.

Typical setting-out work

The agreed scope may include building corners, grid lines, foundation positions, structural reference points, road or infrastructure alignments and design levels. The exact items should come from the project drawings and consultant requirements.

Why drawing control matters

Using superseded or incomplete drawings can create avoidable site problems. The project team should identify the correct drawing revision and clarify discrepancies before critical positions are marked.

Related survey work

Projects may also require a topographical survey before design, engineering survey support during construction, or verification/as-built measurements according to the project requirements.

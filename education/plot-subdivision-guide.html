How to Subdivide a Plot in Zanzibar

Dividing one parcel into smaller plots requires more than drawing lines on a map. The survey requirement depends on the existing parcel information, the proposed division and any applicable planning or statutory requirements.

Important: This is general survey guidance, not an official subdivision approval checklist. Current planning, registration and statutory requirements should be confirmed with the responsible Zanzibar authorities and relevant professional advisers for the individual project.

1. Start with the existing parcel information

Collect the land and survey documents available for the parent plot, together with the site location and approximate area. These provide a starting point for understanding the parcel and determining what survey work may be needed.

2. Explain how you intend to divide the land

Tell the survey team the intended number of plots and, where known, the approximate sizes or intended uses. Access, existing occupation, physical site conditions and project objectives may affect the proposed arrangement.

3. Review the survey scope

The appropriate field and drawing work should be determined from the existing information and intended subdivision. For commercial service information, see our plot subdivision survey support in Zanzibar.

4. Check related survey needs

If the existing plot positions need to be checked first, a boundary verification may be relevant. Where parcel records or another cadastral requirement is involved, see cadastral and land parcel survey support.

If the subdivision is part of a development project, a topographical survey may also be useful for site levels and physical features required by the design team.

What affects the subdivision survey quotation?

Factors can include the size and location of the parent plot, available survey information, number of proposed plots, site accessibility, field conditions, required drawings/deliverables and the professional work needed for the agreed scope.

For this reason, we do not publish one fixed subdivision price for every property.

What should you send us?

Send the Google Maps pin, approximate parent-plot size, available land/survey documents and the number of plots you are considering. If you already have a sketch or development concept, send that as well.

Planning to divide land in Zanzibar?

Send the plot location and available documents. We will review the proposed subdivision and determine the appropriate survey scope before quotation.

Survey Information for Building Projects in Zanzibar

Before design, approvals or construction, your architect, engineer or relevant authority may require measured information about the plot. The exact survey and permit requirements depend on the project and current applicable procedures.

Important: This is a survey guide, not an official building-permit checklist. Planning and permit requirements can change and should be confirmed with the responsible Zanzibar authorities and your architect, engineer or planning professional.

Topographical survey for design

Where site levels, slopes and existing physical features affect the design, a topographical survey can provide measured information for the project team. The required detail should be agreed with the architect or engineer.

Land parcel and boundary information

Depending on the project and available records, the design team may need to understand the relevant plot information. If the immediate concern is checking plot positions on the ground, see boundary verification. For land-parcel survey requirements, see cadastral and land parcel survey support.

Construction setting out

Once approved project drawings are ready and construction is starting, construction setting out transfers the required positions, axes, reference points and levels from the drawings onto the site.

Engineering survey requirements

Larger buildings and infrastructure works may have additional control, level, verification or as-built requirements. These should be defined from the project documents and consultant requirements. See engineering survey support.

What should you send for a quotation?

Send the Google Maps pin/site location, approximate plot size, available land or survey plan, architectural/site drawings if available, and any survey brief or checklist issued by your architect, engineer, consultant or authority.

This helps avoid quoting the wrong survey or promising a document that is not actually required for your project.

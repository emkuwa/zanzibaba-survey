What is construction setting out?

Construction setting out is survey work used to establish the required positions of foundations, grids, columns, walls, roads, drainage and other designed elements on the ground. The exact scope depends on the drawings, control information, construction stage and accuracy requirements.

What information should be available?

Project drawings

Provide the latest relevant architectural, structural or civil drawings. Where coordinates, grids, benchmarks or control points are specified, include that information with the request.

Site location and access

Send the Google Maps pin and explain the current site condition. Access, existing structures and the construction stage affect field planning.

Required points and levels

State whether the requirement is for building axes, foundation positions, columns, road alignment, drainage, levels, external works or another defined scope.

Typical setting out workflow

The surveyor reviews the supplied drawings and available control, confirms the required scope, establishes or checks suitable site control, marks the required points or lines and provides the agreed field information to the project team. Checks and deliverables should be agreed for the assignment rather than assumed.

Setting out and engineering surveys

Setting out is closely related to engineering survey work. Projects may also require a topographical survey before design, boundary information where site limits matter, and as-built measurements during or after construction.

How much does construction setting out cost?

There is no single reliable price for every project. The quotation depends on the site location, drawings, number and type of points, required visits, project stage and agreed deliverables. Send the project information for a scope-specific quotation rather than relying on a generic fee range.

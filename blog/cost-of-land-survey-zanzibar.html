What affects topographical survey cost?

Survey type and purpose

A topographical survey, boundary verification, construction setting out, plot subdivision and engineering survey require different field work and outputs.

Plot size and site conditions

Larger areas generally require more field coverage. Dense vegetation, difficult access, steep terrain, existing buildings or many physical features can increase the work required.

Required detail and deliverables

An architect may require site levels, contours and existing features, while an engineer may specify control, setting-out points, levels or as-built information. The quotation should match the actual brief.

Location and access

The site location in Zanzibar, travel requirements and practical access to the plot can affect mobilisation and field time.
